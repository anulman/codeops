# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CodeOps free from harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept responsibility and correct mistakes.
- Focus criticism on the work and its effects.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials or private data.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. The maintainer can edit or
remove contributions and can temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
The maintainer will protect a reporter's privacy as far as practical.

This policy follows the intent of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
